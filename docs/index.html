What I do.

Anevir is the practice through which I provide professional services, in three areas. I take on new clients by referral.

Business strategy

I look at how work moves through a company and how value reaches its customers. I hold an MBA. I have streamlined operations, automated routine work and built lasting customer relationships.

Clients receive

  • A map of the process, with the slow steps identified
  • Routine work systematized or automated, such as invoicing and reporting
  • Pricing and customer care that match the value delivered

Software engineering

I plan and build software with clients, from the first specification to a system that runs. I also help teams whose projects have stalled find their way back to delivery.

Clients receive

  • A specification that engineers and budget holders both understand
  • A working web application or data tool
  • A faster delivery process

Artificial intelligence

I build my own product with AI tools. I know where they save time and where they need human review. I bring that experience to client teams.

Clients receive

  • An adoption plan tied to work the team already does
  • AI-assisted development workflows that engineers keep using
  • Simple review rules that keep quality high as the team moves faster
