# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plain files, no build step; no tracking, no forms). Published from `docs/` with GitHub Pages. Domain: https://anevir.llc.

## Users

Primary (changed by the owner on 2026-10-08): prospective customers. Consulting clients are organizations deciding about software, open source or AI; Rendity users are long-term IBKR investors. The site must show the value of working with Georg Link and feel lively. It is not written for banks or agencies; company and legal facts stay available but in the background (footer, legal pages).

## Product Purpose

Anevir LLC is a Nebraska limited liability company (Omaha, NE) founded in 2022 by Georg J.P. Link. The site presents Georg as a provider of professional services in business strategy, software engineering and artificial intelligence, who takes on new clients by referral only. It also presents Rendity (rendity.app), his portfolio analytics product for value investors, and Potwin, the AI platform he is building as Chief Information Officer at Growth Advisory (blog: blog.potwin.ai). The site does not solicit contact: no email address or contact form on the main pages; the address appears only on the legal pages.

## Positioning

Two arms, clearly separated: Consulting (services, advisory, engineering) vs Rendity (a software product). Rendity is self-hosted, read-only against IBKR via Flex Query, no third-party analytics, and is analytics only: not investment advice, no custody, no handling of client funds.

## Operating Context

Contact is email only: hello@anevir.llc, no forms. Banks and agencies expect: legal name, entity type, state of formation, principal place of business, registered agent, officers/owners, description of business activities, source of revenue, terms of service, privacy policy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rendity features (from rendity.app): money-weighted IRR (XIRR) per stock and portfolio; income attribution (price gains, option premiums, dividends, interest); options planner; time-weighted benchmark comparison (SPY, QQQ, VOO, any ticker); monthly/yearly heatmaps; German Anlage KAP tax-form fields with loss-offset pools; stock deep dives. Access appears invite-based; pricing not stated. Stack: Django, PostgreSQL, Chart.js.
The street address, EIN and state filing details are deliberately not published on the site or in this repository.

## Brand Commitments

Rendity brand already exists: green (#326848), Literata typeface, "Buy right. Hold tight. Know everything." Anevir LLC has no existing visual identity. Founder bio rests on public facts only (CV at georg.link): Founder and President of Anevir LLC since 2022; Ph.D. Information Technology (UNO, 2019); co-founder of CHAOSS at the Linux Foundation; former banking apprentice/certified Bankkaufmann (2011); MBA, M.S.; adjunct professor; AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner. Prior employer Bitergia (2019-2026) is not a claim of the LLC.

## Evidence on Hand

CV PDF (georg.link/CV-GeorgLink.pdf), rendity.app public landing page (text-based UI mockups; no real image files beyond favicon). No customers, testimonials, revenue figures, or prices: do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Legibility to a compliance reader beats cleverness: entity facts findable in seconds.
2. The two arms never blur; each states what it is and what it is not.
3. Never invent legal identifiers, customers, or claims; mark gaps.
4. Static, fast, no tracking, honest.
